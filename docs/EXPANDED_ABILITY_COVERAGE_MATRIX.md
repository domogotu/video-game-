# Expanded Ability Coverage Matrix

**Status:** Approved design direction; design specification only. No ability is claimed implemented or tested.

## Goal

Lethal Absorption must support the complete intended *range of ability families and gameplay fantasies* established for its six inspiration routes: shadow-commanding hunter, limit-breaking martial artist, adaptive goblin, mythic staff trickster, hidden god-tier slime, and undead spell sovereign. This matrix is the required coverage baseline for all later design and implementation.

This means broad coverage of powers and mechanics across the referenced anime/show and game interpretations, but not a copied move-by-move catalogue. Use original names, lore, visuals, animations, combinations, and mechanics. Do not reproduce proprietary character designs, signature move sequences, dialogue, or storylines.

## Universal ability record

Every ability must have an individual record in `POWER_ABILITY_CATALOGUE.md` defining: effect; action-gated prerequisites; anatomy/body-plan compatibility; resource cost and upkeep; range/area/duration; startup, recovery, and interruptibility; mastery/evolution stages; counters and failure states; combinations; NPC/ecology consequences; PvP and server-authority rules; animation/audio/VFX readability; accessibility; and test cases.

No ability is implemented merely because it appears in this document. No automatic victory, infinite resource loop, unrestricted army/cloning, omniscience, universal immunity, or resurrection of a permanently dead character is permitted. Shared Discovery shares knowledge, not personal unlocks or private information. Sapient species have agency and cannot be treated as interchangeable loot containers.

## 1. Shadow-command sovereign

Required capability coverage:
- Residue reading, presence sensing, threat estimation, and evidence-based trail reconstruction.
- Imprinting bounded echoes from eligible battle traces or remains after a qualifying encounter.
- Echo roster, recall, dismissal, limited recovery, and clear command bandwidth.
- Tactical orders: guard, flank, focus, scout, retreat, screen, interrupt, and regroup.
- Specialized scout, defender, duelist, skirmisher, disruptor, and support roles.
- Formation bonuses, coordinated strikes, limited reinforcement, and emergency substitution.
- Short-range shadow movement, concealment, pursuit, and escape with distance/light/line-of-sight counters.
- Bounded afterimages and decoys; no unlimited independent copies.
- Command growth, echo specialization, tactical archive, territory/domain effects, and late-game sovereign pressure.

Echoes have caps, upkeep, pathing limits, command bandwidth, and counters. They are not resurrected people and cannot track targets omnisciently.

## 2. Limit-breaking martial ascendant

Required capability coverage:
- Stances, light/heavy attacks, parries, dodges, counters, throws, launches, recovery, and combos.
- Energy sensing, charging, suppression, shaping, and controlled release.
- Ranged energy bursts, projectiles, charged attacks, narrow beams, spreads, and area pulses.
- Strength, speed, reflex, durability, jumping, aerial control, and temporary overdrive.
- Aerial pursuit, air movement, interception, anti-air options, and landing recovery.
- Directional guards, timed deflection, brief barriers, and impact dispersal.
- Evidence-based combat reading and pattern punishment, not guaranteed prediction.
- Training, sparring, rivals, drills, recovery, and use-based mastery.
- Staged transformations with distinct benefits, resource demands, and counter windows.
- Telegraphed high-commitment finishers and advanced energy discipline.
- Any future cooperative fusion mechanic requires opt-in consent, a duration, break conditions, and separate balance.

Charge time, fatigue/strain, energy depletion, interruption, and vulnerable recovery must remain meaningful. No form grants universal invulnerability.

## 3. Adaptive goblin reclaimer

Required capability coverage:
- Scent/residue tracking, footprints, resource quality, hidden-path detection, and uncertainty from weather/terrain.
- Selective consumption, digestion, rejection, toxin isolation, and compatibility checks.
- Trait candidates from qualifying encounters, stabilization, limited slots, and meaningful trade-offs.
- Salvage, field crafting, traps, tool/armor/weapon modifications, and consumables.
- Ambushes, bait, escape routes, terrain use, and improvisation after observing an enemy.
- Temporary borrowed instincts, limited by source compatibility and duration.
- Pack signals, role assignments, regrouping, morale, and withdrawal while allies retain autonomy.
- Anatomical adaptations such as claws, armor, sensory organs, gliding, and water tolerance.
- Counter-evolution that grants partial, evidence-based resistance rather than instant immunity.
- Craftmaster, ambusher, pack tactician, scavenger alchemist, duelist, and hybrid branches.
- Meaningful scrap economy and an earned apex form combining survival, craft, and selective traits.

Materials, time, digestion, trait instability, body capacity, and consequences for sapient groups remain relevant. Solo and group strategies must both be viable.

## 4. Mythic staff trickster

Required capability coverage:
- Staff light/heavy strikes, thrusts, sweeps, spinning guard, vaults, disarms, and stance combos.
- Reach/weight/aspect changes with readable telegraphs and recovery.
- Pole-assisted leaps, rebounds, wall redirection, aerial strikes, and terrain-aware landings.
- Feints, false openings, deceptive footwork, cancel windows, and punishable bait.
- Timed deflection, guard-to-counter, and momentum redirection.
- Earned staff aspects for reach, control, mobility, and defense, each with trade-offs.
- Mythic endurance and composure without invulnerability.
- Fragile, short-lived decoys/echoes; no unrestricted autonomous duplicates.
- Long-jump/sky-route traversal with costs and anti-air counterplay.
- Mentor, rival, and boss trials that unlock combo branches and timing mastery.
- Endgame zone control and mobility balanced by commitment and resource use.

Weapon disarm/break rules, collision consistency, limited reach, recovery frames, and feint vulnerability must be specified.

## 5. Hidden god-tier adaptive slime

Required capability coverage:
- Elastic morphology, flattening, compression, squeezing, cushioning, and mass-aware collision.
- Selective assimilation under contact, resistance, size, and compatibility rules.
- Appraisal, analysis, hypothesis testing, and evidence-based certainty rather than omniscience.
- Bounded trait archive, synthesis of compatible traits, and stabilization trials.
- Temporary studied-form echoes without perfect identity impersonation or private-data copying.
- A small number of linked body fragments for scouting or simultaneous tasks, with shared vulnerability and resource limits.
- Re-forming only from a viable core/mass under explicit rules; permanent death remains final.
- Adaptive membrane, partial resistance, and counter-adaptation when attackers vary tactics.
- Organic weapons, tendrils, shields, bindings, ranged droplets, and mobility tools paid for with body mass.
- Selected elemental/energy conduits with incompatibility and overcharge risks.
- Layered barriers, limited internal storage, parallel cognition, threat simulation, and bounded autonomous defense.
- Risk-bearing core fusion, multi-core evolution, sovereign presence, cosmic adaptation, and earned god-tier convergence.
- The three-planet conquest milestone remains required for the specified godlike progression.

No infinite storage, total immunity, perfect copying, free reconstitution, or automatic unlock from one opaque random roll. Hidden route clues and qualifying actions must be discoverable and reproducible.

## 6. Undead spell sovereign

Required capability coverage:
- Spell research from traces, artifacts, enemies, and environments, with hypotheses separated from verified formulas.
- Separate known/prepared/cast/mastered spell states, cast time, concentration, resource, and cooldown.
- Offensive bolts, arcs, area bursts, curses, drains, and delayed zones with clear warnings and counters.
- Layered wards, barriers, dispels, elemental/physical protection, and timed defensive responses.
- Control effects such as slows, binds, disruption, fear-like pressure, and marks with duration limits and resistance.
- Summons and servants with caps, upkeep, command bandwidth, pathing limits, and counterplay.
- Bonecraft, armor/weapon formation, repair, reinforcement, and material-based reconfiguration.
- Ritual sites, traps, alarms, domain effects, and resource anchors that can be discovered, contested, or destroyed.
- Spell archive, tell recognition, counter-scripts, and evidence-based interruption.
- Limited energy extraction with ecological, political, and moral consequences.
- Context-sensitive dread/command presence that does not force-control all sapient beings.
- A late-game domain with high-tier combinations, costs, and vulnerabilities.

Preparation, components, interruption, dispel, summon caps, domain contestability, resistance, and NPC/social consequences are mandatory. Permanent character death cannot be reversed.

## Cross-route combinations

Possible earned combinations include staff combat with short-range shadow movement; martial energy with organic weapons; goblin crafting with bounded echo support; slime morphology with wards; undead spell preparation with scouting echoes; elemental conduits with martial projection; and counter-evolution with any route. These are not free synergies: compatibility, anatomy, resources, timing, readability, and PvP exploitability must be checked. Conflicting abilities may require a choice or impose instability.

## Implementation and acceptance gates

1. Add a distinct original ability record to the power catalogue.
2. Map prerequisites and action gates into the Evolution Atlas.
3. Define costs, range, duration, recovery, counters, and PvP behavior.
4. Specify animation, effects, audio cues, accessibility, and NPC/world consequences.
5. Add unit, interaction, regression, and multiplayer-authority tests.
6. Run tests against actual code and record environment/results before claiming implementation.
7. Keep remaining abilities on the approved roadmap; do not silently omit them when scoping a vertical slice.
