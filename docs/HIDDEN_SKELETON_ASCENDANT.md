# Lethal Absorption — Hidden God-Tier Skeleton Evolution

> **Status:** Detailed design proposal. This is documentation only; no playable implementation or tests are claimed.

## Core concept

Among the game's many monsters and body plans, an apparently ordinary skeleton can secretly evolve into a god-tier undead sovereign. It begins as a fragile, low-threat skeletal creature, not a famous named character or a ready-made endgame build. Players discover the route through rare actions, forbidden ruins, survival feats, magical research, commanding undead, and choices about how they acquire and use power.

The desired inspiration is the broad fantasy of an overwhelmingly powerful undead spellcaster and ruler: deep spell mastery, necromancy, summoning, layered defenses, resistances, battlefield control, preparation, strategic intelligence, a loyal undead host, and a domain that grows with the character. The game's skeleton must have original identity, history, visual design, terminology, abilities, and story. Do not copy Ainz Ooal Gown's character identity, appearance, exact named spells, guild, equipment, scenes, dialogue, or distinctive presentation.

The secret must remain hidden in ordinary character selection and species descriptions. Early clues should resemble rare skeleton adaptations. Only connected discoveries reveal that this lineage can become a world-shaping undead entity.

## Design rules

1. **Weak beginning:** low durability, limited movement, basic melee, and a small chance to survive in dangerous regions only through caution.
2. **Action-gated growth:** unlocks require actions and evidence, not level alone.
3. **Multiple paths:** necromancy, spellcraft, summoning, defense, command, crafting, and domain-building are related branches but not all mandatory at every step.
4. **Mastery is separate:** learning a spell, acquiring it, preparing/equipping it, casting it successfully, and mastering it are separate states.
5. **Undead are not free resources:** summons require eligible sources, control capacity, upkeep, commands, and world rules.
6. **Preparation is gameplay:** scouting, researching enemy weaknesses, setting wards, selecting spells, and shaping the battlefield should reward deliberate play.
7. **The world reacts:** factions can fear, investigate, bargain with, oppose, or seek aid from a rising undead sovereign based on credible evidence.
8. **Power has counters:** sanctified zones, soul-binding effects, anti-magic, command disruption, resource exhaustion, exposed phylactery-like anchors, and rival casters may challenge the player.
9. **No resurrection loophole:** any soul-anchor or reconstitution mechanic must respect Lethal Absorption's permanent character-death rule. It cannot restore a dead player character.
10. **Originality:** this is an original secret lineage using broad undead-overlord fantasy, not a replica of a specific anime character.

## Hidden action-gated progression

Candidate milestone gates; exact thresholds and balance remain open.

| Milestone | Required actions / proof | Candidate unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| 0. Bone Remnant | Survive a dangerous region, scavenge remains, and defeat or escape a low-level threat | Basic skeletal movement, simple melee, low-grade necrotic sensing, resistance to ordinary fatigue |
| 1. Grave Listener | Investigate an ancient burial site, detect a supernatural residue, and correctly identify a threat without simply charging it | Spirit traces, death-energy sensing, improved night perception, first Codex entry about undead ecology |
| 2. Bound Spark | Recover a compatible magical focus or residue, study it, and stabilize it through a ritual or equivalent research challenge | Small resource pool, basic spellcasting, minor ward, first prepared ability slot |
| 3. Bone Architect | Collect and craft compatible remains/materials; build a functioning ward or skeletal tool; survive a counterattack | Bone shaping, armor reinforcement, weapon conjuring from eligible material, repair by consuming resources |
| 4. Grave Binder | Defeat, negotiate with, or investigate an eligible undead source; perform a binding trial and prove control | Summon one weak temporary minion, simple command set, summon duration/upkeep UI |
| 5. Spell Archivist | Discover multiple spell sources, verify their mechanics, and successfully use different spell families under real conditions | Spell archive, spell preparation/loadout, utility/defense/offense specializations |
| 6. Deathless Strategist | Win a difficult encounter using preparation, wards, counters, positioning, and resource management rather than raw stats alone | Advanced analysis, enemy-effect recognition, tactical planning tools, layered defensive magic |
| 7. Crypt Sovereign | Secure a hidden lair, establish protected anchors that are not resurrection devices, and command a controlled group through a multi-part objective | Undead command formations, lair upgrades, minion roles, territory alerts, ritual crafting |
| 8. High Necromancer | Complete a rare research chain and a high-risk ritual with multiple valid solutions; withstand an interruption or hostile counter-ritual | Advanced necromancy, elite summons, battlefield-wide curses, mass control subject to limits, specialized resistance branches |
| 9. Deathless Monarch | Resolve a major faction or apex threat and demonstrate stable control over several different magical systems | Sovereign aura, advanced barriers, command network, powerful transformation, persistent faction/world consequences |
| 10. Beyond-the-Grave Ascendant | Complete the relevant planetary/cosmic progression, research the metaphysics of the setting, and survive a final evolution trial | Cosmic undead adaptation, high-tier magic, interplanetary survival, and eligibility for the game's godlike branches when global prerequisites are met |

A qualifying milestone should support multiple approaches where practical: battle, stealth, research, diplomacy, crafting, or survival. Do not make every step require killing a sapient character.

## Original power suite

Working names; all mechanics need detailed ability records and balancing.

### Necromancy and spirit interaction
- **Grave Sense:** detect nearby death-energy traces and recent supernatural disturbances; it does not reveal all hidden enemies.
- **Remnant Reading:** investigate remains to uncover bounded clues about a creature or event, subject to evidence quality and privacy/story rules.
- **Ossuary Servitor:** assemble a weak temporary skeletal helper from eligible materials; limited commands, duration, upkeep, and vulnerability.
- **Soul Tether:** maintain a bounded link to an eligible summon for commands or shared sensing; disruption and distance can break it.
- **Grave Muster:** call a prepared group of eligible minions from a secured local staging area; no unlimited army spawning.
- **Echo of the Fallen:** create a limited echo from a validated memory/imprint for scouting or a short combat role; not resurrection or a full copy of a person.
- **Sovereign Reaping:** high-tier area effect that harvests eligible ambient necrotic energy or weakens exposed targets, with clear counters and costs.

### Spellcraft and preparation
- **Spell Archive:** records spells actually discovered and researched; knowledge does not equal immediate mastery.
- **Prepared Weave:** select a limited set of spells before combat, with quick access and switching rules.
- **Counter-Script:** after studying an effect, prepare a specific counter or ward; cannot counter unknown abilities automatically.
- **Layered Ward:** stack compatible protective effects, with upkeep and diminishing returns to prevent infinite defenses.
- **Ritual Geometry:** place bounded runes/sigils to shape an area, summon, trap, or defensive field.
- **Mana Conversion:** convert eligible stored resources into a different magical resource at a loss and with capacity limits.
- **Spell Fusion:** combine compatible researched spells into an original technique after a stability trial.
- **Cursed Edict:** apply a visible, resistible debuff or rule to a defined target/area; no unrestricted mind control.

### Undead body and defense
- **Bone Reforging:** repair or reshape eligible skeletal parts using materials and time.
- **Graveplate:** construct layered armor from compatible remains or mineral materials.
- **Deathward Constitution:** acquire specific resistances through research and exposure; not blanket immunity to all physical or magical damage.
- **Stillness of the Crypt:** reduce sound and detectable presence while stationary, with counters based on senses and evidence.
- **Soul-Fire Mantle:** a high-tier defensive aura that consumes energy and can be disrupted by defined counter-effects.
- **Phylactery Principle:** research a lore concept about distributed anchors or stored patterns, but never use it to resurrect the player's permanently dead character. If used for NPCs or bosses, define explicit discoverable vulnerabilities and persistence rules.

### Command, lair, and sovereign powers
- **Legion Discipline:** assign roles and formations to a capped number of eligible minions based on command bandwidth.
- **Crypt Domain:** upgrade a lair that supports crafting, research, recovery, minion management, and local defenses; it requires resources and can be found or contested.
- **Dread Presence:** influence eligible creatures through fear or supernatural pressure; resistance, motivation, range, and faction context matter.
- **Sovereign Decree:** issue a strong tactical command to bound summons or allies; it does not override another player's agency.
- **Grave Network:** connect discovered wards, scouts, or lair nodes for limited alerts and travel support; it cannot provide omniscience.
- **Undead Evolution:** evolve eligible summons into specialized roles after materials, research, and upkeep investments; each branch has weaknesses.
- **Cosmic Sepulcher:** late-game transformation that allows survival in hostile planetary/space environments and high-tier magical combat.
- **God-Crown Convergence:** secret ascension branch unlocked only through the world's late-game conditions, including applicable three-planet conquest requirements; powerful but still subject to world, resource, and multiplayer rules.

## Discovery and secrecy

- The ordinary skeleton entry should list only its visible low-tier characteristics.
- Early secret clues appear as unusual resistance, strange reactions to old ruins, rare spell residues, or unexpected compatibility with necrotic materials.
- The player must connect evidence from multiple sources; a single random drop must not be the only path.
- Once a condition is discovered, the Codex records the evidence and the Atlas can track known prerequisites.
- The full endgame route remains hidden until enough clues are connected.
- Shared Discovery Network records may provide hints, but must not automatically grant the personal achievements, spell mastery, summon control, or lineage unlock.
- The secret should be reproducible after players understand its rules, not dependent on an opaque random chance.

## Evolution Atlas and UI

Each spell, summon, ritual, resistance, transformation, and domain upgrade must show:
- State: unknown, discovered, researched, eligible, acquired, prepared, mastered, active, exhausted, or unavailable.
- Known action prerequisites and progress evidence.
- Source, compatibility, target eligibility, costs, duration, range, upkeep, and control bandwidth.
- Counters, interrupt conditions, failure modes, and environmental requirements.
- Minion count/capacity, command latency, autonomy, persistence, and recovery rules.
- Whether the ability is optional, mutually exclusive, respec-able, or part of a hidden branch.
- Feedback explaining why an action failed after the relevant rule has been discovered.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A new skeleton starts weak and does not see the god-tier destination at character creation.
2. Every major unlock is tied to logged actions, discoveries, trials, or evidence.
3. Spell discovery, acquisition, preparation, successful casting, and mastery are distinct.
4. Summons cannot exceed their resource, command, target, and persistence limits.
5. Advanced magic offers counters and readable warning signals in PvE and PvP.
6. The character can become an exceptionally powerful undead sovereign without copying a franchise character.
7. The secret is discoverable through reproducible actions once the player understands the clues.
8. Permanent player-character death remains permanent.
9. NPC and faction reactions use credible evidence and world knowledge.
10. Implementation and testing status are recorded separately from this design document.

## Open decisions

- Whether this lineage is a secret starting species, a rare evolution of ordinary skeletons, or both.
- Whether spell acquisition uses a universal mana system, multiple magical resources, or world-specific systems.
- Maximum simultaneous summon/control bandwidth and how it scales.
- Whether the Crypt Domain is personal, guild-shared, discoverable, or contestable.
- Which spells and summon behaviors belong in the first playable slice.
- How the route intersects with NG+, permanent death, the Evolution Vault, and player legacy NPCs.
- Exact numeric costs, cooldowns, spell slots, resistance limits, and stage thresholds.
