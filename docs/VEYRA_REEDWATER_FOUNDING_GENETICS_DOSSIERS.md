## 1. Rules for this first pass

Each species has five **candidate heritable modules**, not five proven genes. A module can represent multiple genes or alien developmental factors. Observed body traits are not evidence of simple Mendelian inheritance. Do not infer dominance, recessiveness, or compatibility without a reproductive mechanism and population evidence. All allele models, mutation ranges, and numeric balance values remain TBD. Learned behaviors, player abilities, and environmental conditions are tracked separately.

Statuses: **D** = feature supported by existing dossier; **H** = heritability hypothesis; **U** = reproductive or inheritance pathway unknown. All module records have H inheritance status unless explicitly noted.

## 7. Gameplay genetics and ecosystem integration

- A player may study morphology, sensory cues, life stages, egg placement, or colony regrowth without breeding or killing a specimen.
- An inherited module is not automatically an unlocked player skill. Absorption research follows its own compatibility and mastery gates.
- Damage to reeds can remove Reed Skipper egg shelters; sediment compaction can reduce Silt Burrower egg survival; scraping Glassfilm reduces biomass; drying threatens Null Slime. These effects must be driven by local patch state.
- Trait-based ecological roles are contextual: Silt Burrower mixing may aid sediment renewal in one patch while harming water clarity in another.
- Never spawn new offspring, egg clusters, or biofilm from nothing; reproduction consumes resources and requires surviving viable populations.
- Player discovery UI distinguishes visible phenotype, inferred heritability, confirmed reproduction, and unknown genetics.
