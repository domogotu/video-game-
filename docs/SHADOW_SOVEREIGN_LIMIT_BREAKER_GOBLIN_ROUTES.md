# Lethal Absorption — Original Shadow Sovereign, Limit-Breaker, and Goblin Evolution Routes

> **Status:** Design proposal only. These are original routes built from broad genre qualities associated with *Solo Leveling*, *Dragon Ball*, and *Re:Monster*. They are not licensed characters, copied movesets, or implemented gameplay.

## Design intent

Add three distinct character fantasies to the existing action-gated evolution framework:

1. A hidden **Shadow Sovereign** route: a seemingly unremarkable survivor gradually earns the ability to command an army of echo-like entities.
2. A **Limit-Breaker Martial Ascendant** route: a combat-focused organism grows through training, meaningful rivals, escalating challenges, and high-risk transformations.
3. A **Goblin Reclaimer** route: a weak goblin evolves through survival, selective consumption, skill learning, crafting, social bonds, and strategic adaptation.

The references describe the broad appeal the user wants, not permission to copy the original protagonists, their names, exact powers, visual designs, lore, dialogue, costumes, or signature presentation. Final characters, ability names, forms, animations, and world history must be original.

## Route A — Hidden Shadow Sovereign

### Core fantasy

Begin as a low-ranked, overlooked, or physically unimpressive survivor. Repeatedly survive encounters that should have ended the run, investigate unusual traces left by defeated threats, and earn a concealed path toward a sovereign who can deploy a force of shadow-like echoes. The route is not revealed in character creation or granted simply by leveling.

### Action-gated discovery chain

- Survive a dangerous encounter while outmatched, using positioning or an environmental advantage rather than a scripted rescue.
- Investigate a rare battlefield residue and preserve evidence instead of immediately consuming or selling it.
- Complete a solo survival trial in a location where group support is unavailable or limited.
- Learn the difference between an ordinary remains imprint and a stable echo-compatible imprint.
- Defeat an eligible powerful opponent under the required conditions, then perform a costly binding/echo trial.
- Demonstrate control discipline by maintaining a small echo group through a multi-stage encounter without exceeding command bandwidth.
- Secure a unique personal milestone that reveals the Sovereign branch in the Evolution Atlas.
- Late-game ascension requires the established planetary/cosmic progression gates; three-planet conquest remains a prerequisite for the relevant godlike absorption tier.

These gates are a proposed structure. Exact targets, order flexibility, costs, and stage thresholds remain open for balancing.

### Original working ability family

- **Umbral Trace** — detect residual energy from significant recent events; traces can be misleading or decayed.
- **Echo Imprint** — study a qualified remnant to determine whether an echo can be formed.
- **Vow of the First Echo** — high-cost trial that binds the first temporary echo if all conditions are met.
- **Dusk Retinue** — command a limited number of echoes within a defined control radius or network.
- **Reserve Recall** — withdraw a controlled echo from danger at an energy and cooldown cost; this does not revive the player.
- **Sovereign Formation** — coordinate echo roles such as interception, pursuit, scouting, or disruption.
- **Gloom Exchange** — swap position with an eligible echo only when line-of-sight, space, and counterplay rules permit.
- **Marching Archive** — retain research notes about eligible echoes, not their full mastery or independent personhood.
- **Crown of the Last Light** — late-stage transformation that improves command capacity while imposing severe upkeep and vulnerability windows.
- **Eclipse Dominion** — cosmic-tier ability candidate, available only after the relevant endgame conditions and with explicit counters.

### Constraints and counterplay

- Echo count, strength, duration, command bandwidth, distance, upkeep, and recovery must be explicit.
- Powerful echoes require meaningful acquisition conditions and should not be farmed from trivial targets.
- Echoes can be disrupted, resisted, separated, outmaneuvered, or forced to retreat according to their individual rules.
- Scouting echoes must not reveal hidden player data or bypass privacy/fog-of-war rules.
- NPCs can only infer the player's power from witnessed behavior, evidence, reports, or learned information.
- The route's flavor cannot bypass permanent character death. No shadow, anchor, echo, or stored imprint resurrects the player's dead character.

## Route B — Limit-Breaker Martial Ascendant

### Core fantasy

A fighter with no guaranteed cosmic destiny builds strength through disciplined training, technique, recovery, exploration, and worthy opponents. Transformations emerge from learned control and hard-earned thresholds, not from a copied alien bloodline or a guaranteed rage button. The player can specialize in hand-to-hand combat, ki-like bioenergy, aerial pursuit, defense, counterattacks, or balanced mastery.

### Action-gated discovery chain

- Learn basic movement, guard, evade, and strike timing against opponents with readable tells.
- Complete repeated training objectives with measurable improvements in control, not just time spent attacking a dummy.
- Survive a high-pressure encounter with resource management and defensive play.
- Study an unfamiliar energy phenomenon and safely reproduce a limited technique.
- Win against a rival by adapting to their pattern rather than relying only on higher stats.
- Unlock a first controlled overdrive after proving recovery, restraint, and energy control.
- Reach later transformations through distinct feats: technical mastery, environmental adaptation, team challenge, and a high-tier solo trial.
- Cosmic or godlike progression remains tied to the shared planetary/cosmic rules, including the three-planet condition where applicable.

### Original working ability family

- **Pulse Step** — short burst movement with stamina/energy cost and a punishable recovery window.
- **Impact Thread** — chain strikes when timing and target state permit.
- **Aether Guard** — briefly reinforces the body against a telegraphed hit, but cannot block every attack.
- **Rising Vector** — launches or pursues a target based on mass, footing, and momentum.
- **Focused Wave** — charged ranged energy release with interruption risk and readable telegraph.
- **Stillpoint Read** — studies an opponent's repeated attack rhythm; loses value when the opponent changes tactics.
- **Limit Surge** — temporary output increase with heat, fatigue, or post-use vulnerability.
- **Skyline Pursuit** — controlled aerial chase whose performance depends on body plan and atmosphere.
- **Horizon Breaker Form** — advanced transformation with altered movement and energy handling, not a free win.
- **Star-Heart Discipline** — late-stage control route that rewards precise use and restraint over raw output.

### Constraints and counterplay

- Transformations change options and trade-offs, not simply multiply all stats without limit.
- Charging creates a clear risk window; interrupts, dodges, cover, range, and timing can counter it.
- Repeated overdrive causes defined strain and recovery costs.
- A higher level or transformation does not automatically defeat a skilled player of comparable combat scale.
- Avoid copied Saiyan biology, Super Saiyan naming/visual cues, exact beam attacks, iconic poses, character silhouettes, or signature scene recreations.

## Route C — Goblin Reclaimer

### Core fantasy

Start as a fragile goblin with limited equipment, low status, and few safe options. Grow through survival and community: hunt intelligently, consume only compatible material, learn from encounters, craft tools, develop a role within a group, and evolve into a distinctive goblin lineage that may eventually surpass much larger species. This is an evolution-and-skill route, not an automatic “eat anything, gain everything” shortcut.

### Action-gated discovery chain

- Survive the opening region by scavenging, hiding, tracking, negotiating, or hunting small prey.
- Identify and prepare safe food/material rather than consuming every specimen raw.
- Craft a first tool or piece of protective gear from locally available resources.
- Learn a technique by observation and practice; recording a technique does not grant mastery.
- Build trust with a small group or deliberately choose a solitary survival strategy, with different benefits and risks.
- Complete a hunt that rewards planning, evidence handling, and retreat decisions.
- Discover compatibility between a consumed trait and the goblin's current anatomy.
- Choose an evolution specialization such as scout, duelist, trapper, pack tactician, alchemist, or adaptive hunter.
- Earn advanced forms through proven skill, rare ecological discoveries, and meaningful encounters, not only accumulated kills.
- Any godlike extension follows the established endgame and three-planet requirements.

### Original working ability family

- **Scrapwise** — identify likely uses and hazards in salvaged materials.
- **Scent Ledger** — track scent trails while accounting for weather, water, competing trails, and target countermeasures.
- **Quickcraft** — assemble simple field tools from compatible materials; quality depends on supplies and practice.
- **Borrowed Instinct** — temporarily improve one learned behavior after analysis of a compatible creature, with reduced performance outside its context.
- **Pack Signal** — communicate a limited tactical cue to consenting or allied group members within suitable conditions.
- **Gut Filter** — reduce risk from some ingested substances; does not make unknown toxins universally safe.
- **Ambush Geometry** — gain a situational advantage from terrain, concealment, and preparation.
- **Trait Stitching** — combine compatible minor traits at a cost, with mutation risk and opportunity cost.
- **Goblin Apex Branch** — choose an advanced specialization based on the player's actions and community history.
- **Green-Crown Evolution** — a rare late-stage form that joins adaptive biology, learned skills, and group strategy without erasing the goblin route's distinct identity.

### Constraints and counterplay

- Consumption rewards are narrow, eligible, and compatibility-checked; no automatic full skill or memory theft.
- Crafting depends on actual materials, time, tools, and local knowledge.
- Social growth must respect NPC agency; allies are not disposable stat bonuses.
- The route can support solo or group play, but neither path should be strictly mandatory without a deliberate design decision.
- A goblin evolution can become powerful without being a one-to-one copy of the *Re:Monster* protagonist or its exact progression.

## Shared secret-route rules

1. The high-tier destination stays hidden from character creation and early UI.
2. Clues must be discoverable and the gate sequence reproducible after players understand it; avoid pure opaque random rolls.
3. The Evolution Atlas reveals only what the character has observed, researched, or earned. Shared Discovery shares verified knowledge, not personal unlocks.
4. Each ability entry must specify source, effect, prerequisites, anatomy compatibility, cost, range, duration, cooldown/recovery, counters, failure modes, combinations, and world/NPC consequences.
5. Secret routes do not bypass permanent death, PvP fairness, server authority, privacy, or established planetary/cosmic progression.
6. Each route needs its own silhouette, motive, animation language, visual effects, weakness profile, and narrative footprint.
7. Separate design approval from implementation and verification; no feature is considered implemented until code and tests demonstrate it.

## Open decisions

- Whether each route is a playable starting lineage, a hidden branch reachable from multiple lineages, an NPC/rival route, or a combination.
- Exact resource model for echoes and energy-based martial abilities.
- Whether the goblin route has required group/social gates or fully viable solo alternatives.
- Exact thresholds, costs, cooldowns, stage placements, rarity, and PvP tuning.
- Which milestones belong in the first playable vertical slice.

**Current status:** Design proposal. No route, ability, animation, NPC, or gameplay implementation is claimed to exist or to have passed tests.
