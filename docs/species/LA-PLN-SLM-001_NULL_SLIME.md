# Lethal Absorption — Species Dossier: Null Slime

> **Stable ID:** LA-PLN-SLM-001  
> **Planet:** Veyra  
> **Status:** Designed — dossier integration of the foundational Slime blueprint; not implemented or tested  
> **Authority:** `docs/SLIME_MASTER_CHARACTER_BLUEPRINT.md` remains the master for the life path and locked starting-form decisions.

## 1. Identity and design role

**Working species name:** Null Slime  
**Temporary high-stage codename in the master blueprint:** Null Sovereign  
**Role:** Player-capable, limbless adaptive organism; the foundational playable lineage for the Veyra vertical slice.

“Null” describes the organism's initially limited sensory and anatomical capabilities, not emptiness, immunity, or supernatural negation. The starting body is vulnerable and must earn every meaningful advantage. Null Slime is a lineage with an evolutionary life path, not a collection of unrelated classes.

## 2. Base body and starting stages

### Stage 0 — Dormant cell
- Tiny single cellular mass; no limbs, conventional eyes, mouth, skeleton, lungs, or separate hands.
- Movement is limited to membrane contractions and passive drift. No standard jump, sprint, or attack.
- Survival loop: detect a safe nutrient cue, pulse toward it, absorb tiny particles, stabilize the membrane, and avoid environmental hazards.
- Principal threats include drying, extreme temperatures, toxins, violent currents, crushing pressure, and larger organisms.

### Stage 1 — Primitive Slime
- One soft mass with a central nutrient core and outer membrane; no limbs.
- Movement includes contracting, flattening, rolling, crawling/oozing through gaps, short compression hops, and brief clinging to suitable surfaces.
- Surface climbing is limited by texture, moisture, slope, body condition, stamina, and load. It is never a universal wall-climb.
- Early offense is weak body contact, adhesive contact, and engulfing tiny resources—not a conventional melee combo.
- Early defense includes compressing under some attacks; piercing, drying, heat, and membrane disruption remain dangerous.

Stages are milestones, not fixed level brackets. Rare discoveries may change access order, but a new branch still requires compatibility, cost, mastery, and counterplay.

## 3. Locked sensory design

**Mostly blind at the start.** Stage 0 and Stage 1 do not have conventional image-forming vision. Initial information comes primarily from chemical sampling, contact/pressure, and vibration transmitted through connected ground or fluid.

- **Chemical sampling:** Can suggest nutrient traces, toxins, familiar biological signatures, and possible trails. Wind, water, contamination, competing traces, and unfamiliar chemistry can mislead it.
- **Vibration/contact:** Can suggest direction, proximity, and intensity of movement through connected ground or fluid. Soft terrain, airborne movement, environmental noise, and slow deliberate movement can reduce usefulness.
- **Ambient light/temperature:** May be registered only as crude non-visual cues. Default thermal vision is not available.
- **Manual sensory switching:** The player chooses which available sensory interpretation to emphasize. Switching changes presentation, not the world state, and cannot reveal a target without a valid sensory path.
- **Distinct eye branches:** Different eye-bearing species can unlock different visual evolution branches. Absorbing one eye-bearing species does not grant universal vision or every eye-derived power.
- **No omniscience:** Opaque walls block direct sight; through-wall information is limited to physically plausible transmitted cues. Sensory confidence and environmental interference should be communicated.

## 4. Movement, condition, and terrain

Movement is a consequence of body shape, surface conditions, and resources rather than a generic humanoid controller.

- Crawl/ooze through gaps according to body size and opening geometry.
- Short hops require suitable membrane condition and available stamina; they are not sustained flight.
- Limited surface climbing requires compatible texture and moisture conditions and is affected by slope and load.
- Water flow can assist or endanger movement; currents, mud, dry stone, contamination, and steep surfaces have different handling.
- Injury, fatigue, nutrient state, membrane damage, and acquired traits can change performance.
- Splitting, merging, long-range tendrils, strong adhesion, acid/enzyme attacks, and advanced shape-shifting are later branches, not starting abilities.

## 5. Feeding, absorption, and progression

Eating, safely digesting, analyzing, adapting, and mastering are separate steps. Contact with a target does not automatically transfer all of its abilities.

**Early safe loop:**
1. Detect a potential nutrient or biological trace.
2. Assess whether the source appears safe using available cues; uncertainty remains possible.
3. Approach using terrain-appropriate movement.
4. Absorb only targets/resources permitted by current size and capability.
5. Process the material and recover from any cost or adverse reaction.
6. Analyze the result and pursue a compatible adaptation.
7. Practice and master the unlocked capability under its constraints.

**Live prey:** Active live-prey absorption is a contest, not an instant kill. During an active drain, target health decreases over time according to relevant capabilities, mastery, target health, defense/resistance, size, condition, and compatibility. The target can struggle and break free; escape stops ongoing drain. Death occurs only when legitimate game-system health resolution reaches zero. Stamina matters but is not the only determinant.

**Limits:** Internal capacity, energy, heat, stability, attention, anatomy, and loadout constrain simultaneous powers. Absorption is not immunity to toxins, damage, or incompatible biology. Trait conflicts and failed adaptations must have readable causes and recovery options.

## 6. Habitat and starting-region link

**Primary starting range:** Reedwater Basin, especially First Drip Hollow, Reedwater Run, Mossbank Edge, and the margins of Reedglass Marsh. Quietroot Grotto is a possible later refuge, not a guarantee of safety.

Null Slime is not assumed to be naturally common in every Veyra biome. Moisture, food, temperature, salinity, contamination, and local predators constrain distribution. Dry or exposed regions require later adaptations or alternate routes.

**Opening progression:** Stage 0 cell → Stage 1 Primitive Slime. The first region should teach sensing, safe feeding, movement, and evasion before demanding sustained combat.

## 7. Threats, behavior, and recovery

- Early behavior is simple stimulus-response: follow a plausible nutrient cue, withdraw from damaging conditions, shelter when depleted, and avoid threats it can detect.
- The Slime does not automatically identify predator species from a vague signal; it infers danger from movement, chemistry, and past experience.
- Ambushes are possible when a predator exploits a sensory blind spot, but important first-time hazards should have learnable clues and reasonable recovery.
- Tutorial-stage setbacks must be recoverable before the core movement, absorption, and evasion lessons have been taught.
- PvP cannot permanently kill the player or steal assets/progression. PvP defeat, recovery, and competitive consequences must respect the game's protection rule.

## 8. Candidate abilities and learning sources

These are progression candidates governed by the skill atlas; they are not all available at start.

| Candidate capability | Earliest rationale | Cost / limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Chemical Trace Reading | Repeatedly compare nutrient, toxin, and organism traces | Misleading mixtures; environmental interference |
| Vibration Interpretation | Practice reading movement through connected ground/fluid | Weak on soft, noisy, airborne, or disconnected paths |
| Adhesive Contact | Study suitable surfaces and develop membrane control | Surface-dependent; can be disrupted |
| Engulf Tiny Resources | Demonstrate safe intake and processing | Small targets only; digestion has limits |
| Membrane Stabilization | Survive and recover from minor membrane stress | Consumes resources; does not prevent all damage |
| Compression Hop | Practice safe short hops after Stage 1 body control | Stamina and landing risk |
| Limited Surface Cling | Learn on compatible textures | Moisture, slope, load, and stamina limits |

A capability needs a linked skill record, prerequisite, learning source, practice condition, mastery stages, counterplay, and failure behavior before implementation.

## 9. Evolution and cross-species compatibility

Null Slime may explore many biological branches, but compatibility is evaluated per trait. A creature's entire anatomy or behavior is not copied by absorption.

- Eye-derived branches depend on the specific eye-bearing species and compatible sensory anatomy.
- Heat tolerance, toxin processing, armor, locomotion, aquatic adaptation, and predatory structures each need their own biological rationale.
- Conflicting traits may compete for capacity or destabilize the body; adaptation can require staged practice.
- No trait should silently remove all early vulnerabilities. Strong late-game forms remain readable and counterable.
- Cosmic and space-survival branches occur only after planetary progression; the Living Ship path is biological rather than a conventional vehicle.

## 10. Resources and nonlethal learning

Early progression should include nonlethal observation and environmental study. The player can learn from tracks, chemical traces, feeding sites, discarded material, and habitat changes when available. Absorption is one learning path, not the only route to every fact or objective.

Resource harvesting must account for ecological renewal. Removing a major food source or stripping a biofilm patch should have consequences; the world must not generate unlimited biomass on demand.

## 11. AI and state contract

Minimum conceptual state groups for future implementation:
- **Body:** life stage, size band, membrane integrity, injury, stability, available traits.
- **Resources:** nutrient reserve, stamina, digestion load, energy/heat burden.
- **Senses:** available modes, current selected mode, cue confidence, environmental interference.
- **Behavior:** explore, sample, approach, feed, retreat, shelter, recover, practice.
- **Evolution:** prerequisites, compatibility evidence, candidate traits, mastery, conflicts.
- **Persistence:** recoverable defeat state, progression protection, saved evolution state.

The runtime must not equate a design dossier with a working AI. These state groups require schema, behavior implementation, and tests.
