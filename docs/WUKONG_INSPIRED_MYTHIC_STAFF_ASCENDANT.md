# Lethal Absorption — Mythic Staff Ascendant (Wukong-Inspired)

> **Status:** Original design proposal only. This route draws on broad mythic qualities associated with Sun Wukong and modern Wukong games/anime: staff mastery, agility, trickery, transformation, defiance, and mythic trials. It is not a reproduction of a particular copyrighted game's or show's character, plot, visual design, dialogue, or exact move set. No gameplay is claimed to be implemented.

## Availability requirement

**Wukong-inspired gameplay must be available in Lethal Absorption.** Treat this as an approved design direction for the concept roster: players should be able to pursue a playable mythic staff-fighter route, and the world may also feature original staff-wielding rivals, mentors, and bosses. Whether the route is available from character creation or must be earned through a hidden branch remains an explicit design decision; it must not be omitted from the planned roster.

The final playable character must have an original name, species/background, silhouette, proportions, costume/armor, staff design, animations, effects, voice, lore, and signature techniques. Use the inspiration as a direction, not a one-to-one reskin of Sun Wukong or any specific game's interpretation.

## Core fantasy

A mobile, technically demanding staff fighter combines reach, footwork, aerial movement, feints, counterattacks, and controlled transformations. The route rewards timing, positioning, enemy-reading, and improvisation. Its mythic feeling comes from a difficult journey through trials and from a staff that evolves with the wielder, not from copying a recognizable character's exact presentation.

## Playable route and progression

### Early stage — Wandering Staff Adept

- Begin with a modest staff, evasive footwork, short combos, and deliberate counters.
- Learn staff reach, leverage, spacing, and terrain interactions.
- Discover training shrines, forgotten techniques, and rival combat schools through exploration.
- The staff is a physical weapon with weight, reach, durability/energy rules, and recoverable animations—not a universal answer to every enemy.

### Mid stage — Trickster Disciple

- Unlock feints, vaults, spinning defense, aerial redirection, and staff-assisted traversal.
- Earn transformation-adjacent abilities through trials, research, and skill milestones.
- Use decoys or afterimage-like distractions only under explicit duration, range, and counterplay rules.
- Improve technique through correct execution and mastery, not simply repeated button presses.

### Advanced stage — Mythic Staff Ascendant

- Unlock a longer-reach staff form with different speed and recovery trade-offs.
- Develop a small set of original transformation options: a brief evasive shift, a size/weight modulation technique, or a limited decoy technique, each with clear limits.
- Complete a sequence of mythic trials requiring different skills: duel, navigation, deception detection, endurance, and restraint.
- Cosmic and godlike extensions remain subject to the game's established late-game and three-planet progression rules where applicable.

## Original working ability family

- **Wayfarer's Staff** — baseline melee chain with distinct light, heavy, guard, and counter branches.
- **Reedstep** — fast lateral movement that costs stamina and can be punished if overused.
- **Skyvault** — staff-assisted leap or traversal move constrained by anatomy, surface, and momentum.
- **Turning Guard** — rotating defense against certain directional attacks; vulnerable to grabs, area effects, and timing breaks.
- **False Opening** — a feint that can bait eligible enemy reactions; smart opponents may adapt after observing it.
- **Echo Feint** — short-lived visual misdirection, not a full autonomous clone and not a way to bypass server-authoritative targeting.
- **Long-Reach Aspect** — temporarily alters staff reach and handling, trading speed and recovery for spacing.
- **Stone-and-Wind Rhythm** — a mastery branch that rewards alternating grounded strikes and aerial movement.
- **Trial-Bound Aspect** — a temporary transformation earned through a specific personal trial, with a defined cost and vulnerability window.
- **Heaven-Road Mastery** — a late-stage technique family for players who master several branches; it does not guarantee victory or ignore counters.

All names are placeholders and must undergo originality review.

## Combat identity

- **Strengths:** adaptable reach, mobility, feints, counterattacks, environmental traversal, strong skill expression.
- **Weaknesses:** commitment during heavy strikes, stamina/energy pressure, limited value when trapped in unsuitable terrain, vulnerability to well-timed area control and ranged pressure.
- **Mastery:** spacing, rhythm changes, defense-to-offense transitions, and reading an opponent's habits.
- **Counterplay:** punish recovery, deny safe vault surfaces, vary attack timing, use area denial, track decoys by reliable tells, and pressure resource depletion.
- **Build freedom:** staff techniques can be combined with compatible anatomy, movement, and defensive abilities; combinations must obey costs and interaction rules.

## World and character roles

The route can support:
- A playable player-evolution branch.
- An original wandering staff mentor who teaches selected techniques after demonstrated skill.
- Rival staff fighters with different philosophies and readable weaknesses.
- A mythic trial boss whose challenge tests movement, restraint, observation, and combat mastery rather than inflated health alone.
- Optional lore and discovery chains about ancient staff disciplines and transformations.

The mentor/rival/boss content must have its own motives, culture, relationships, and narrative consequences. Do not treat the reference as a license to copy exact story events or recognizable dialogue.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The route is explicitly included in the planned roster and is not accidentally dropped from future feature planning.
2. It supports a playable staff-focused build, with an explicit decision still needed on starting availability versus hidden unlock.
3. Core techniques have costs, tells, recovery, counters, and anatomy/terrain rules.
4. Transformations and decoys are bounded and server-authoritatively validated in multiplayer.
5. The visual, narrative, animation, and move design is distinct from any one source adaptation.
6. Player death remains permanent; no transformation or mythic artifact resurrects the player character.
7. The implementation status is tracked separately from design approval.

## Open decisions

- Is the route available as a starting combat discipline, an earned hidden branch, or both?
- Is the staff a selectable weapon category, an evolved body-organ/tool, or both?
- Which transformation mechanics fit the first playable slice?
- Exact resource costs, cooldowns, weapon durability/energy rules, and PvP balance.
- Which original mentor, rival, or boss introduces the route?
