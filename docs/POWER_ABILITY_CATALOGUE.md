# Lethal Absorption — Master Power and Ability Catalogue

**Status:** Initial breadth catalogue; design inventory only, not implemented or balanced. No finite list can include every conceivable power; new concepts and emergent combinations can extend it. This is an original design reference organized around broad genre conventions from games, anime, television, comics, mythology, fantasy, science fiction, and speculative biology—not a reproduction of franchise-specific characters or signature implementations.

## Catalogue rules
- Separate **source**, **effect**, **delivery**, **target**, and **modifier**. Example: a biological organ generates heat, a focused beam delivers it, and a mutation extends range.
- Treat generation, control, absorption, conversion, resistance, immunity, sensing, and movement as separate abilities when their gameplay differs.
- Every ability needs defined effects, eligible targets, range, duration, cost, prerequisites, counters, failure states, and multiplayer rules.
- A listed concept is not automatically available to every anatomy or stage. Use cellular, primitive, main creature, transformation, spacefaring, cosmic/planetary, and godlike versions as appropriate.
- Combinations should be compositional and data-driven. Unknown combinations can be hypotheses until tested and recorded in the Codex.

## AD. Hybrid combination templates
- Fire + wind: spread, acceleration, firestorm, controlled flame shaping.
- Water + electricity: conductive zone or chain discharge; manage ally and self-risk.
- Ice + water: freezing, armor, terrain control, pressure effects.
- Earth + plant: root-bound terrain, living fortification, accelerated growth.
- Metal + magnetism: weapon retrieval, armor shaping, projectile control.
- Sound + structural sensing: resonance weak-point discovery.
- Gravity + projectile: curved trajectory, pinning, enhanced impact.
- Gravity + teleportation: spatial displacement with added aiming/energy constraints.
- Shadow + stealth: concealment or shadow movement with light/detection counters.
- Light + illusion: optical decoys or refractive camouflage.
- Regeneration + extra limbs: recovery support with energy/tissue limits.
- Venom + delivery limb: anatomy-specific toxin attack.
- Winged dive + tendril anchor: dive, anchor, swing, release into flight.
- Armor + electricity: conductive defense or risky shock counter.
- Fire organ + claws: heated strikes with heat-transfer/self-damage rules.
- Life + death: reanimation/soul/restoration effects only if metaphysics and costs permit.
- Time + movement: acceleration or short temporal step with anti-loop safeguards.
- Space + force: bounded spatial barrier, displacement, or cutting effect.
- Matter shaping + biology: adaptive organs/living tools with resource costs.
- Cosmic energy + regeneration: high-tier repair with major costs and counters.
- Psychic + swarm: shared sensing/coordination limited by control bandwidth.
- Absorption + analysis + compatibility: informed selective mutation rather than automatic copying.

## AE. Standard full ability record
For every selected ability, capture:
- **ID/name; family/subfamily; status** (concept, researched, verified discovery, designed, implemented, tested).
- **Exact effect and game-state outcome; source/mechanism; delivery; eligible targets.**
- **Applications; unlock/evolution prerequisites; anatomy/organ/body-plan compatibility.**
- **Resource, range, duration, cooldown, recovery, environmental dependencies.**
- **Counters, resistances, failure modes, interactions, combination rules.**
- **Visual/audio/animation feedback; NPC/world-state consequences; multiplayer validation and anti-exploit rules.**
- **Lower-stage version, advanced evolution path, Codex evidence, attribution, open questions, acceptance criteria, and test cases.**
